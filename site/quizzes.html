See where you stand.

Free quizzes built on the measures we use in our research. Each one takes a few minutes and gives you your own results.

What Kind of Curious Are You?

24 questions, about 5 minutes. Based on the Five-Dimensional Curiosity Scale Revised. You get a profile across six dimensions: joyous exploration, deprivation sensitivity, stress tolerance, thrill seeking, overt social curiosity, and covert social curiosity.

Take the curiosity quiz

Answers are anonymous and help our research. The curiosity quiz uses Kashdan, Disabato, Goodman, & McKnight (2020), Personality and Individual Differences.
